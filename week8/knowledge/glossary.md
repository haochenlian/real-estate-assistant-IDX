# Real Estate Glossary

## Days on Market (DOM)
Days on Market, abbreviated DOM, is the number of days between the date a property is
listed for sale and the date it goes under contract. A low DOM means homes are selling
quickly and the market favors sellers; a high DOM means listings are sitting and buyers
have more leverage. DOM is one of the clearest signals of how competitive a local market
is right now.

## List Price
The list price is the price a seller is publicly asking for a property when it goes on
the market. It is the seller's opening position, not necessarily what the home is worth
or what it will ultimately sell for.

## Close Price
The close price, also called the sale price, is the amount the property actually sold
for when the transaction closed. It reflects what a buyer was genuinely willing to pay
after negotiation.

## List-to-Close Ratio
The list-to-close ratio is the close price divided by the list price, expressed as a
percentage: (ClosePrice / ListPrice) x 100. A ratio below 100% means homes are selling
for less than asking, so buyers have room to negotiate. A ratio at or above 100% means
homes are selling at or over asking, indicating a competitive seller's market. For
example, a home listed at $800,000 that sells for $780,000 has a list-to-close ratio of
97.5%, meaning the buyer negotiated 2.5% off the asking price.

## Price Per Square Foot
Price per square foot is the close price divided by the living area in square feet. It
allows fair comparison between homes of different sizes. Note that price per square foot
is not constant across home sizes: larger homes typically sell for less per square foot
than smaller homes, the same way buying in bulk lowers the unit price.

## Comparable Sales (Comps)
Comparable sales, commonly shortened to comps, are recently sold properties similar
enough to a subject property that their sale prices serve as a fair benchmark for
valuing it. Good comps share the same city or neighborhood, a similar size (typically
within 20% of the subject's square footage), and a similar property type. Appraisers and
agents use comps to determine whether a listing is priced above, below, or in line with
the market.

## Active Listing
An active listing is a property currently for sale and available to receive offers. In
the MLS data, this is indicated by a status of "Active".

## Pending
A pending property has an accepted offer but the sale has not yet closed. It is no
longer available for new offers under normal circumstances.

## HOA Fee
A homeowners association fee is a recurring charge paid by owners in a managed community
or condominium building. It covers shared maintenance and amenities. HOA fees are
reported monthly in the MLS data.

## MLS
The Multiple Listing Service is the shared database real estate professionals use to
publish and access property listings within a region. The data in this project comes
from California MLS records.

## RESO
The Real Estate Standards Organization defines standardized field names for real estate
data. Field names such as ClosePrice, ListPrice, BedroomsTotal, and LivingArea follow
the RESO standard.

## Seller's Market vs. Buyer's Market
In a seller's market, demand exceeds supply: days on market are low and the
list-to-close ratio is at or above 100%. In a buyer's market, supply exceeds demand:
days on market are high and homes sell below asking price.
